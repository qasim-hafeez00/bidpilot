# Content recommendation engine

## Context
Readers left after one article. The client was a media company.

## What I did
Two-tower retrieval plus a re-ranker, served behind a cache. I worked mainly with Python, Recommendation Systems, Machine Learning. Under the hood the system relied on an in-memory cache and message broker, a relational database.

## Outcome
The project pages per session up 22%.
