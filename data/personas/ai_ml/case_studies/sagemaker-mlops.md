# MLOps pipeline on SageMaker

## Context
Models were trained in notebooks and deployed by hand. The client was a fintech company.

## What I did
Built training, registry and blue-green deployment pipelines with automated checks. I worked mainly with MLOps, AWS SageMaker, AWS, Python. Under the hood the system relied on a reproducible container image, automated test-and-deploy pipelines, an experiment tracker and model registry.

## Outcome
The project release time cut from two weeks to one day.
