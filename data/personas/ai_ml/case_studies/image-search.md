# Visual product search

## Context
Shoppers could not find products from photos. The client was a e-commerce company.

## What I did
CLIP embeddings with an approximate nearest-neighbour index. I worked mainly with Python, Computer Vision, Deep Learning. Under the hood the system relied on a managed similarity-search index, a typed async Python web service, a deep learning framework.

## Outcome
The project search-to-cart rate up 15%.
