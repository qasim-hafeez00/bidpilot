# WhatsApp appointment bot for clinics

## Context
Clinic receptionists handled hundreds of booking messages a day. The client was a healthcare company.

## What I did
Intent and slot extraction bot integrated with the clinic calendar via the official API. I worked mainly with Python, Chatbot, NLP. Under the hood the system relied on a lightweight Python web app, an in-memory cache and message broker.

## Outcome
The project 62% of bookings completed without staff.
