# Multi-agent research assistant

## Context
Analysts spent days compiling competitor briefs. The client was a market research company.

## What I did
Planner, researcher and critic agents with tool use and source-cited outputs. I worked mainly with Python, LLM, LangChain, RAG. Under the hood the system relied on a managed similarity-search index, an in-memory cache and message broker.

## Outcome
The project brief turnaround from 3 days to 4 hours.
