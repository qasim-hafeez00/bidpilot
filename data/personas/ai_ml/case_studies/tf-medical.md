# Chest X-ray triage classifier (research)

## Context
A research lab wanted a triage baseline for public X-ray data. The client was a healthcare company.

## What I did
Transfer learning with careful patient-level splits and calibration. I worked mainly with Python, Deep Learning, TensorFlow, Computer Vision. Under the hood the system relied on vectorised array maths.

## Outcome
The project AUROC 0.87 on the held-out split; published as a reproducible notebook.
