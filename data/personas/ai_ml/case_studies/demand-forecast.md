# Weekly demand forecasting for 600 SKUs

## Context
Stock-outs and overstock on fast-moving products. The client was a retail company.

## What I did
Built hierarchical forecasts with gradient boosting and calendar features, retrained weekly. I worked mainly with Python, Time Series Forecasting, pandas, scikit-learn. Under the hood the system relied on a scheduled DAG orchestrator, a relational database.

## Outcome
The project MAPE improved from 31% to 18%.
