# Steel surface defect detection

## Context
Manual inspection missed hairline cracks on a production line. The client was a manufacturing company.

## What I did
Labelled 4,300 images, trained a detector, and deployed it on an edge box. I worked mainly with Python, Computer Vision, PyTorch, YOLO. Under the hood the system relied on classical image pre-processing, a reproducible container image.

## Outcome
The project recall on cracks rose from 72% to 94%.
