# Meeting transcription and summarisation

## Context
Consultants lost action items from long client calls. The client was a consulting company.

## What I did
Transcribed with Whisper, diarised speakers, summarised into action items with an LLM. I worked mainly with Python, Speech Recognition, LLM, NLP. Under the hood the system relied on a reproducible container image, a background worker queue for slow jobs.

## Outcome
The project summaries ready 3 minutes after a call ends.
