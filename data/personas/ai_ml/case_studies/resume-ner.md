# Resume parsing with named-entity recognition

## Context
Recruiters re-typed candidate details from PDF resumes. The client was a HR tech company.

## What I did
Fine-tuned a token classifier on 2,000 annotated resumes and wrapped it as an API. I worked mainly with Python, NLP, Hugging Face. Under the hood the system relied on a deep learning framework, a typed async Python web service.

## Outcome
The project field-level F1 of 0.89; data entry time halved.
