# Review sentiment analysis

## Context
A hotel chain could not track guest complaints across 40 properties. The client was a hospitality company.

## What I did
Aspect-based sentiment model over 120k reviews with a weekly report. I worked mainly with Python, NLP, Machine Learning. Under the hood the system relied on dataframe tooling, charts for stakeholders.

## Outcome
The project identified two recurring service issues that were then fixed.
