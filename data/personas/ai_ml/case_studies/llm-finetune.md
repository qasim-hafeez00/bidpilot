# Domain fine-tune of an open LLM for insurance emails

## Context
Generic models wrote off-tone replies to policy holders. The client was a insurance company.

## What I did
Fine-tuned a 7B model with LoRA on 9k approved replies and evaluated with a rubric judge. I worked mainly with Python, Fine-tuning, LLM, Hugging Face. Under the hood the system relied on a deep learning framework, cloud infrastructure.

## Outcome
The project agents accepted 64% of drafts unchanged.
