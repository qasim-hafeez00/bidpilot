# Subscription churn prediction

## Context
A SaaS company could not tell which accounts were about to cancel. The client was a SaaS company.

## What I did
Engineered usage features, calibrated a classifier, explained drivers with SHAP. I worked mainly with Python, Machine Learning, scikit-learn, pandas. Under the hood the system relied on an experiment tracker and model registry.

## Outcome
The project the retention team saved 140 at-risk accounts in a quarter.
