# Two-sided services marketplace

## Context
A founder needed an MVP to match homeowners with tradespeople. The client was a home services company.

## What I did
Listings, bookings, escrow-style payments and reviews. I worked mainly with Next.js, TypeScript, Node.js, PostgreSQL, Stripe. Under the hood the system relied on an in-memory cache and message broker, a reproducible container image.

## Outcome
The project launched in 9 weeks; 1,800 bookings in the first quarter.
