# Booking web app with calendar sync

## Context
A gym chain took class bookings by phone. The client was a fitness company.

## What I did
Booking SPA with Google sign-in and calendar sync. I worked mainly with React, Firebase, Authentication. Under the hood the system relied on a shared spreadsheet.

## Outcome
The project phone bookings down 80%.
