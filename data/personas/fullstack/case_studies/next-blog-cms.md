# Marketing site and CMS

## Context
An agency's marketers needed to publish without developers. The client was a agency company.

## What I did
Headless CMS with preview, i18n and image optimisation. I worked mainly with Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, HTML/CSS. Under the hood the system relied on a typed query layer.

## Outcome
The project publishing time from 2 days to 15 minutes.
