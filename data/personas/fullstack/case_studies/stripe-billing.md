# Subscription billing integration

## Context
An edtech platform billed manually by invoice. The client was a edtech company.

## What I did
Plans, trials, proration and dunning via Stripe webhooks. I worked mainly with Stripe, Django, React. Under the hood the system relied on a background worker queue for slow jobs, a relational database.

## Outcome
The project involuntary churn down 23%.
