# Front-end performance audit

## Context
A news site's pages took 7 s to become interactive. The client was a media company.

## What I did
Code splitting, image strategy and performance budgets in CI. I worked mainly with React, JavaScript, HTML/CSS. Under the hood the system relied on automated test-and-deploy pipelines.

## Outcome
The project Time-to-interactive from 7.1 s to 2.3 s.
