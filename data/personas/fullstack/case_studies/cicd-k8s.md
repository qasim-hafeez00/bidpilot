# CI/CD and Kubernetes migration

## Context
Deploys were manual SSH sessions with downtime. The client was a SaaS company.

## What I did
Containerised 6 services, GitHub Actions pipelines, rolling deploys on EKS. I worked mainly with Kubernetes, Docker, CI/CD, AWS. Under the hood the system relied on a relational database.

## Outcome
The project zero-downtime deploys, 15 per week.
