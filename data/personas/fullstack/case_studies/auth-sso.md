# SSO and role-based access control

## Context
Enterprise clients demanded SSO before signing. The client was a enterprise software company.

## What I did
OIDC/SAML SSO with SCIM provisioning and fine-grained roles. I worked mainly with Authentication, Node.js, React. Under the hood the system relied on an in-memory cache and message broker, a relational database.

## Outcome
The project unblocked 3 enterprise deals.
