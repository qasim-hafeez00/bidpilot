# Delivery tracking mobile app

## Context
Customers called restaurants to ask where their order was. The client was a food delivery company.

## What I did
Live map tracking with push notifications. I worked mainly with React Native, TypeScript, Firebase. Under the hood the system relied on live push updates.

## Outcome
The project support calls about order status down 45%.
