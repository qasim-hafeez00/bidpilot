# BigQuery cost and query optimisation

## Context
A media company's warehouse bill tripled in a year. The client was a media company.

## What I did
Partitioning, clustering and materialised summary tables. I worked mainly with BigQuery, SQL. Under the hood the system relied on version-controlled SQL transformation models, a free BI reporting tool.

## Outcome
The project monthly warehouse cost down 46%.
