# Marketing attribution dashboard

## Context
The brand could not see which channels drove repeat buyers. The client was a D2C brand company.

## What I did
Multi-touch attribution views blending ad spend and orders. I worked mainly with Tableau, SQL, Data Visualization. Under the hood the system relied on a serverless cloud warehouse, web analytics events.

## Outcome
The project reallocated 20% of budget, CAC down 14%.
