# Sales performance dashboard

## Context
Regional managers waited a week for sales reports. The client was a wholesale company.

## What I did
Star-schema model, DAX measures and row-level security per region. I worked mainly with Power BI, DAX, SQL, KPI Reporting. Under the hood the system relied on spreadsheet exports.

## Outcome
The project report refresh daily instead of weekly; used by 45 managers.
