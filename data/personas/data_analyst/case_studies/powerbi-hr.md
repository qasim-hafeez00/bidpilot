# HR attrition analytics

## Context
HR could not explain rising attrition. The client was a manufacturing company.

## What I did
Attrition drivers, tenure curves and headcount forecasts. I worked mainly with Power BI, DAX, Data Analysis. Under the hood the system relied on warehouse queries, spreadsheet exports.

## Outcome
The project targeted retention plan for 2 departments with highest risk.
