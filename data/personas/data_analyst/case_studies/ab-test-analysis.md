# Checkout A/B test analysis

## Context
A team shipped checkout changes on gut feel. The client was a e-commerce company.

## What I did
Power analysis, sequential testing guardrails and a readout template. I worked mainly with A/B Testing, Statistics, Python, pandas. Under the hood the system relied on warehouse queries.

## Outcome
The project identified a variant with +3.1% conversion (p=0.01).
