# ETL pipeline for ad platforms

## Context
Analysts downloaded CSVs from 5 ad platforms daily. The client was a marketing agency company.

## What I did
Scheduled extract-load jobs with schema checks and alerting. I worked mainly with ETL, Python, SQL. Under the hood the system relied on a scheduled DAG orchestrator, a serverless cloud warehouse, a reproducible container image.

## Outcome
The project saved 10 analyst hours per week.
