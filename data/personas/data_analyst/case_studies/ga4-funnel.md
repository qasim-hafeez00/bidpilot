# GA4 funnel and web analytics audit

## Context
Sign-up tracking was broken after a site redesign. The client was a SaaS company.

## What I did
Event taxonomy, funnel exploration and a QA checklist. I worked mainly with Google Analytics, Data Analysis, Looker Studio. Under the hood the system relied on a serverless cloud warehouse.

## Outcome
The project fixed tracking for 14 events; trial sign-ups attributable again.
