# Operations KPI board in Looker Studio

## Context
Dispatch managers tracked on-time rates by hand. The client was a logistics company.

## What I did
Automated data feeds and a live KPI board. I worked mainly with Looker Studio, Google Sheets, KPI Reporting. Under the hood the system relied on a serverless cloud warehouse.

## Outcome
The project on-time delivery visible hourly; late deliveries down 9%.
