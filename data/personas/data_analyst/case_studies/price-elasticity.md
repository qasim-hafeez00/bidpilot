# Price elasticity study

## Context
A grocer priced by competitor matching only. The client was a grocery company.

## What I did
Log-log regression by category with promotion controls. I worked mainly with Statistics, Python, Data Analysis. Under the hood the system relied on dataframe tooling, classic ML estimators.

## Outcome
The project recommended price changes worth an estimated 2.2% margin.
