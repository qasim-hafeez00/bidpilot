# Customer cohort retention analysis

## Context
Leadership did not know when customers churned. The client was a subscription box company.

## What I did
Cohort tables and survival curves by acquisition channel. I worked mainly with SQL, Data Analysis, Data Visualization. Under the hood the system relied on a free BI reporting tool, a serverless cloud warehouse.

## Outcome
The project found month-3 drop-off that led to a new onboarding email series.
