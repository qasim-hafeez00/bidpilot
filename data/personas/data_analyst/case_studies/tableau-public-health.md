# Public health indicators dashboard

## Context
A nonprofit's reports were 60-page PDFs nobody read. The client was a nonprofit company.

## What I did
Interactive district-level dashboard with accessible colour palette. I worked mainly with Tableau, Data Visualization, Data Cleaning. Under the hood the system relied on a statistics language.

## Outcome
The project monthly views up from ~0 to 2,300.
