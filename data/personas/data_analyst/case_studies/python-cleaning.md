# Customer master data cleanup

## Context
A CRM held 300k customer records with duplicates. The client was a insurance company.

## What I did
Fuzzy matching, address standardisation and merge rules with review queues. I worked mainly with Python, pandas, Data Cleaning. Under the hood the system relied on vectorised array maths, warehouse queries.

## Outcome
The project removed 41k duplicates with 99% precision on an audited sample.
