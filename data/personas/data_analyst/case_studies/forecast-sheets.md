# Sales forecasting model in Google Sheets

## Context
Founders needed a board-ready revenue forecast. The client was a SaaS company.

## What I did
Driver-based model with scenarios and a seasonality baseline. I worked mainly with Google Sheets, Statistics, KPI Reporting. Under the hood the system relied on seasonal baselines.

## Outcome
The project forecast within 4% of actual for 3 consecutive quarters.
