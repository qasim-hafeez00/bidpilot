# Analytics engineering with dbt on Snowflake

## Context
Metric definitions disagreed across teams. The client was a SaaS company.

## What I did
Layered staging/marts models with tests and documentation. I worked mainly with dbt, Snowflake, SQL, ETL. Under the hood the system relied on a scheduled DAG orchestrator, automated test-and-deploy pipelines.

## Outcome
The project one source of truth for 38 KPIs; 140 dbt tests.
